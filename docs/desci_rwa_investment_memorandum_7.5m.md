## Section 2 — Legal Architecture & Howey Compliance

### 2.1 Why This Is Not a Security

The Howey Test establishes four prongs for a transaction to constitute a security: (1) investment of money, (2) in a common enterprise, (3) with expectation of profits, (4) derived from the efforts of others.

Genesis Live Core dismantles prong (3) and (4) through the **Outcome-as-a-Service model**:

- **Tier 1 Node Holders** purchase computational validation rights — they run SiLA 2 validator nodes and receive 70% of platform margin *as a fee for services rendered*, not as passive investment income.
- **Tier 2 DeSci Syndicate** acquires dedicated laboratory stream access — a prepaid B2B service contract for IP-NFT experimental throughput, not an equity stake.
- **Tier 3 Community Pass** provides 4K zero-latency stream access and ×2 voting weight — a functional utility, not a profit-sharing instrument.

**No participant receives returns derived solely from the efforts of the issuer.** Returns are contingent on the participant's active role in the validation network or consumption of laboratory services.
