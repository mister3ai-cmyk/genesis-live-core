# Benchmark note: Gr(4) encoder on LongMemEval_S

> 2026-11-22 · applies to ENCODER_EVAL_RESULTS.md and results/ in this directory

- **Split.** Every figure comes from the LongMemEval_S dev split only:
  135 questions (150 minus the `_abs` ones). The test split has not been run.
- **Status: preliminary.** Under the decision rule fixed before the run
  (TZ §6), the dev outcome is "B. Indistinguishable": G1 minus the best
  multi-vector baseline is 0.0 to +1.5 pp of turn recall_any@5 across four
  model/dimension settings, and every 95 % bootstrap CI contains 0.
  The decision is made only on test.
- **Faces.** F1/F2 were generated by Claude Haiku (prompt f12-v1); F0/F3 are
  the raw turn and its neighbours.
- **No optical scoring.** All scoring here is real-valued float32 inner
  products computed on a CPU. No optical or photonic hardware or simulation
  was involved.
- **No complex phase.** No complex-valued or phase-encoded representation
  was implemented or measured. Nothing in these results argues for or
  against optical or phase-based scoring.
